
# rTechCoding AI Sales Platform

**rTechCoding** is an all-in-one AI sales platform designed to give your sales team every tool needed to automate, engage, and grow—from finding leads to closing deals.

## 9 Powerful Modules
 
* **01. Find & Enrich Leads with AI**: Discover ideal prospects and enrich them with accurate data.
* **02. Lead Scoring**: AI scores and ranks leads by buying intent.
* **03. Sell & Run Campaigns**: Create, automate, and run multi-channel campaigns that get replies.
* **04. Every Channel, One Inbox**: Manage all conversations across WhatsApp, Email, Calls, SMS & more — in one shared inbox.
* **05. Book & Manage Appointments**: Read calendars, book best slots, send confirmations and reminders automatically.
* **06. Surveys, Read by AI**: Create surveys and let AI analyse sentiment, themes and NPS instantly.
* **07. Knowledge Base**: Centralise your knowledge and let Sara answer instantly — policies, prices, templates & more.
* **08. Reports & Analytics**: Track revenue, leads, conversion and campaign performance with live dashboards.
* **09. Lead Management**: Pipeline view from discovery to closed deal with AI-suggested next steps and follow-ups.
* **10. Customer Management**: $360^\circ$ customer view with order history, health scores, conversations and preferences.
